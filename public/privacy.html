Squishbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Squishbox is a collecting game for kids. It is built so that it does not need to know anything about the person playing it.

What we collect

Nothing. Squishbox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no email, no name, no location, and no device identifiers.

What stays on the device

Your collection, coins, streak, parent PIN, and the activity log are saved only on the device the app runs on. They never leave it. Deleting the app, or using "Reset everything" in the Parent corner, erases them.

Advertising and analytics

Squishbox has no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind, first-party or third-party.

Purchases

Squishbox has no purchases. Coins are free and are given once a day inside the app. The odds for every box are shown before it is opened.

Children

Squishbox is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
