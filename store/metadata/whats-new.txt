# 1.4
Your buddy has a whole world now. It picks somewhere to go and wanders off on its own — around its room, out to the garden, into the kitchen — and turns things up while it explores. Put food in the bowl and it comes over when it notices.

Once a day it comes home from exploring with coins for you.

Other people's dumplings are round for the day too. Tap one to say hello.

And the Snowy Steamer opens on December 1st: eight frosty dumplings, in the basket until January 5th.
