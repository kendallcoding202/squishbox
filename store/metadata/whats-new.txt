# 1.4.1
A fix for a serious problem: after the last update, some players opened Squishbox and found their collection gone.

Your dumplings are now kept safe in two places, and the app checks both before it ever starts a new game, so an update cannot empty your basket again.

We are very sorry if this happened to you. This update cannot bring a lost collection back, but it makes sure it does not happen again.
