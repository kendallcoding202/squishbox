# 1.5
Every dumpling has been redrawn. All 66 of them, from Bao to Northstar, are now soft, squishy and shiny, and they still squish when you press them.

Your buddy's room, the garden and the kitchen are painted too, with a proper bowl and cup to fill, and the baskets in the shop have had a makeover.

Nothing else has changed: your collection, coins and buddy are all exactly where you left them.

The Snowy Steamer opens on December 1st.
